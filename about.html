About

I am a marine ecologist at the University of the Balearic Islands (Department of Fundamental Biology and Health Sciences). I completed my PhD in Marine Ecology at the same university in 2021, with a thesis on the quantification, ingestion and threat of plastic pollution on marine diversity, graded Excellent Cum Laude.

My research follows marine litter through the ecosystem. I have mapped its spatial and temporal distribution in aquaculture zones and Marine Protected Areas such as Cabrera National Park, and built risk assessments of plastic pollution for the Mediterranean at regional and national scales. My work on microplastic ingestion in commercially important fish showed that long-term exposure in species like gilthead sea bream can trigger oxidative stress, inflammation and behavioural change, and that microplastics can carry pollutants such as organochlorine pesticides.

More recently I have widened the lens to cumulative pressures on coastal ecosystems: invasive species like the blue crab Callinectes sapidus, marine heatwaves, pathogenic Vibrio, and the use of sponges and sea urchins as bioindicators.

I teach in the BSc in Biology at UIB, sit on the committee of the Master in Marine Ecology, and co-supervise undergraduate and master's students. I care about statistical rigour and have organised training courses on zero-inflated, Bayesian and spatial models in R.
